# Haaylo redesign: the mockup rollout

## Where we are

You made two mockups (the app layout and the calendar) because the app felt confusing. They are now the blueprint for everything below. Nothing in this plan changes how content is generated, saved, or approved. It is a layout and workflow redesign only.

Already done:
- New 9-item menu: Home, Campaigns, Strategy, Create, Calendar, Emails, Insights, Brand, Settings. Old pages still work, they just are not in the menu.
- Slide-in post editor (right-hand panel): edit text with character counts, add or generate an image, pick a date and time, Save / Approve & schedule / Duplicate / Move to drafts, with an Undo message. Live in the Content Bank.
- Work packages: everything you generate is saved as one titled package, not a loose pile of posts.

## The plan, in order

### 1. One editing experience everywhere (in progress)
The slide-in post editor becomes the only way you edit a post, on every screen.
- Wire it into Home ("Needs your review") and into each campaign's Posts tab.
- Editing anywhere updates the same post, so calendar, campaigns and packages stay in sync. No copies.

### 2. Home becomes your morning command centre
- Monday Brief at the top: what worked last week, what changed in your plan, drafts ready for you, and "Approve all" in one click.
- Competitor Move card: when a competitor does something, Haaylo suggests a counter-angle with Save as draft / Add to plan / Dismiss.
- "Needs your review" list: everything waiting on you, one click to open the editor.

### 3. Campaign Hub
Each campaign gets a calm one-screen workspace:
- "Getting this campaign live" checklist (connect page, schedule posts, send emails).
- Five tabs: Posts, Emails, Page (edit inline, desktop/mobile preview, one-click publish), Images, Results.

### 4. Strategy
- Tabs for 90-day plan, 30-day plan and Audience.
- "What changed, and why" strip so you can see what Haaylo adjusted.
- Weekly cards with a short reason for each topic and a "Create this week" button.

### 5. Calendar (from your calendar mockup)
- Week view as a time grid (7am to 9pm), with green bands showing your best posting times and a line for "now".
- A Drafts shelf on the side: drag a draft onto a slot to schedule it, with an Undo message.
- Month view toggle and channel filters (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook).
- "Auto-schedule" button that fills the week for you.
- Clicking any post opens the same slide-in editor.

### 6. Create
Seven quick tools on one screen: Write a post, Idea Generator, Rewrite, Hashtags & Keywords, Trending Scan, Competitor Scan, Image Generator. Everything you make drops into the active campaign or a package, never loose.

### 7. Emails
Visual builder: To / From / Subject at the top, blocks you can edit inline, desktop and mobile preview, Send test, Schedule.

### 8. Insights
Tabs: Overview, Competitors, Trends, Leads. "What Haaylo learned" section showing what was applied to your plan. Top posts get "Boost as ad" and "Repeat this format".

### 9. Settings
Autopilot toggle, connected social accounts, lead automations, and permission rules for what Haaylo may do without asking.

### 10. Live Agent heartbeat
A small pill fixed at the bottom-right on every page showing what the agent is doing. Click it to open an activity drawer: "What I did, and why", with timestamps, and a chat box.

## What I will not touch
- Generation, brand grounding, compliance, approvals, packages, database, sign-in. The redesign only changes how screens look and how you move between them.
- Ads stays out until it exists.

## How we'll work
One step at a time, in the order above. After each step I'll show it to you on screen before moving on. You say "go" or tell me what to adjust.
